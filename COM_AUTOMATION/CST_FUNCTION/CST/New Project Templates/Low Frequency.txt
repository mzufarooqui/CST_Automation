Useful for time-harmonic and general time-dependent problems which have to consider eddy currents, skin depth and/or proximity effects or displacement currents
 
Typical applications: NDT, proximity sensors, inductively coupled power transfer, induction heating, high voltage insulators, switch gears

Units: mm, Hz
Boundaries: electric
Background: normal
Mesh: optimized for low frequency structure
  (Tetrahedral: min. number of steps = 5
   Hexahedral: ratiolimit=50, minimum line number=20)
